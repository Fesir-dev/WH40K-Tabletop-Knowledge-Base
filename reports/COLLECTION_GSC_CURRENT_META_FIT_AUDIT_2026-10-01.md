## Practical conclusion

The collection is already **strongly playable for infantry-centric GSC** and is particularly well suited to Acolyte/Xenocult-style construction.

It is not yet copy-depth complete for the most common Biosanctic + Xenocult tournament spine.

The next physical audit should not count more Acolyte bodies. It should count:
1. heavy mining tools
2. demolition charges
3. Metamorph arm sets
4. Neophyte seismic-cannon sets
5. Neophyte cult icons
6. Ridgerunner weapon/support modules
7. Rockgrinder primary weapon modules

Only after those counts is it useful to optimize a specific 2000-point list from the collection.
