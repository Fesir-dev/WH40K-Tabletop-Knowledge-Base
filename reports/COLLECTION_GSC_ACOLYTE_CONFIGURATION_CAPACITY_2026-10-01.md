## Leader constraint

The historical uploaded roster contained five separate Acolyte units:
- two Autopistol units
- three Hand-Flamer units

Therefore at least **five simultaneous leader configurations** are historically evidenced.

Splitting the 45 bodies into more than five Acolyte/Metamorph units may require a direct check of leader-compatible bio-weapon parts/bodies.

## Collection conclusion

The Acolyte collection is not body-limited for current competitive construction.

The limiting resources are:
1. Heavy Mining Tools
2. Metamorph mutation components
3. leader configurations if splitting into more than five units
4. specialist Demolition Charge allocation only for demo-heavy lists

The guaranteed stock already supports the principal current competitive Acolyte/Metamorph patterns with substantial reserve.
