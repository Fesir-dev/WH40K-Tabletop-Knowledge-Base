## Data-model rule

The collection distinguishes:

1. physical model/body count;
2. current fixed assembled loadout;
3. immediately swappable magnetized loadout;
4. loose spare bits/components;
5. theoretical future conversion work.

A theoretical future conversion is **not** recorded as current loadout availability.

New Recruit remains provenance/configuration evidence and is not physical collection authority where direct user inspection exists.
