## Outcome

Direct physical inspection now establishes exact aggregate totals for both Skitarii Battleline pools:

- Skitarii Rangers: **24 physical models**
- Skitarii Vanguard: **14 physical models**

These totals supersede the New Recruit roster quantities of 30 Rangers and 20 Vanguard as collection authority. The source roster remains preserved as configuration/provenance evidence only.

### Residual count conflict

An earlier direct count stated 13 galvanic-rifle-only Rangers. If the later exact total of 24 is authoritative and the above categories are mutually exclusive, then:

24 - 2 Alpha - 3 arquebus - 3 plasma - 3 arc - 1 tether - 1 omnispex = 11

Therefore the database does **not** silently change 13 to 11. It records the discrepancy as an unresolved user-confirmation gate.

14 - 2 Alpha - 3 plasma - 1 arquebus - 0 arc - 1 tether - 1 omnispex = 6

A plausible explanation is that the earlier seven included the Alpha holding a radium carbine. This is recorded as a possible resolution, not as an assumed fact.

## Physical-build model

The Skitarii pool is a historical WYSIWYG configuration library, not a set of permanently defined squads.

User recollection:

- collection construction began around 7th edition;
- models were built over multiple rules eras;
- alternate glued bodies were created to satisfy changing tournament WYSIWYG requirements;
- approximately two plastic Skitarii kits were used;
- additional cast-copy bodies were fitted with remaining plastic bits/arms;
- body-by-body provenance between plastic and cast copies is not known.

The cast/plastic origin statement remains recollection rather than verified per-model provenance.

## Current 11E capacity context

Current 11E mirror data uses 10-model Ranger/Vanguard units with one Alpha and nine ordinary Skitarii. It allows up to one arc rifle, one plasma caliver and one transuranic arquebus replacement per unit; a rifle/carbine bearer can additionally carry either enhanced data-tether or omnispex.

Under that structure:

- 24 Rangers + 2 Ranger Alphas -> at most **2 complete 10-model Ranger units simultaneously**, with 4 bodies available as alternate WYSIWYG configurations.
- 14 Vanguard -> at most **1 complete 10-model Vanguard unit simultaneously**, with 4 bodies available as alternates.
- 3 Ranger copies of each special weapon exceed the simultaneous need of two current Ranger squads by one body per special weapon.
- 3 Vanguard plasma-caliver bodies exceed the simultaneous need of one current Vanguard squad by two bodies.
- no physical Vanguard arc-rifle body exists; that option is therefore unavailable without conversion/new model, but it is optional rather than required.

This section is contextual physical-capacity analysis against the current mirror and does not replace normative current-rules authority.
