# Adeptus Mechanicus Skitarii physical reconciliation — pass 1 — 2026-09-30

## Purpose

Separate New Recruit roster selections from direct physical observation for Skitarii Rangers and Vanguard.

## Ranger comparison

The imported roster contains three 10-model Ranger units:

1. Alpha + arc rifle + plasma caliver + transuranic arquebus + data-tether + 5 galvanic-rifle bodies.
2. Alpha + 9 galvanic-rifle bodies.
3. Alpha + arc rifle + plasma caliver + transuranic arquebus + data-tether + 5 galvanic-rifle bodies.

Therefore the roster snapshot selected:

- 3 Alpha;
- 2 arc rifle;
- 2 plasma caliver;
- 2 transuranic arquebus;
- 2 data-tether;
- 19 other galvanic-rifle bodies.

Direct visual recount instead confirms:

- 3 transuranic arquebus;
- 3 plasma caliver;
- 3 arc rifle;
- 1 data-tether;
- 1 omnispex;
- 13 galvanic-rifle-only bodies.

The physical collection therefore contains variants not represented by the selected roster configuration. Direct visual evidence takes precedence for physical inventory.

## Vanguard comparison

The imported roster contains two 10-model Vanguard units:

1. Alpha + plasma caliver + transuranic arquebus + data-tether + 6 radium-carbine bodies.
2. Alpha + 9 radium-carbine bodies.

The roster snapshot therefore selected:

- 2 Alpha;
- 1 plasma caliver;
- 1 transuranic arquebus;
- 1 data-tether;
- 15 other radium-carbine bodies.

Direct visual recount confirms so far:

- 3 plasma caliver;
- 1 data-tether;
- 1 omnispex;
- 7 standard radium-carbine-only bodies.

Arc-rifle, transuranic-arquebus, Alpha and remaining Vanguard bodies are still pending direct classification.

## Current 11E implementation context

The current 11E roster implementation represented by the imported New Recruit data, cross-checked against the current Wahapedia mirror, uses 10-model Ranger/Vanguard units with one Alpha plus nine ordinary Skitarii; the modeled options permit at most one arc rifle, one plasma caliver and one transuranic arquebus, plus either an enhanced data-tether or omnispex on an ordinary rifle/carbine bearer.

This is contextual implementation/mirror evidence used only to explain why spare physical bodies/configurations are useful. It does not redefine the physical inventory or promote mirror evidence to normative rules authority.
