## Data-model decision

The canonical inventory records shared physical chassis, not every possible current datasheet projection.

Current ally limits, datasheet mapping, legal weapon combinations and points belong to the current rules/points layer.

This prevents magnetized alternate configurations from being double-counted as ownership.
