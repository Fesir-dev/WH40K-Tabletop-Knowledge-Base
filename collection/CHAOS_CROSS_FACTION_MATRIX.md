# Chaos cross-faction collection matrix

Checkpoint: **2026-09-30**

This document answers two different questions separately:

1. **What physical Chaos models are actually owned?**
2. **Which current 11E armies can legally use those bodies/datasheets?**

A shared rules role never creates another physical miniature.

## Global Chaos collection accounting

Current normalized cross-domain known total:

**667 unique physical bodies/models**

Breakdown:

- Chaos Space Marines — **155 confirmed unique bodies**
- Thousand Sons — **114 normalized bodies** (109 in the historical source export; Rubrics directly corrected from 15 to 20)
- World Eaters — **48 unique bodies**, excluding the shared Spawn pool
- Chaos Daemons — **340 normalized bodies**
- shared Chaos pool — **10 Chaos Spawn**

If the remembered separate ten-model Legionaries box is physically reconfirmed, the CSM total becomes 165 and the global Chaos sum becomes **677**.

Verification depth is not uniform: Chaos Daemons/Khorne and several CSM layers have direct physical reconciliation, while Thousand Sons and parts of World Eaters still rely on source-export evidence.

## 1. Chaos Space Marines as a hub faction

### Cult of the Dark Gods

At Strike Force size, CSM can include up to **500 pts combined** of:

- Khorne Berzerkers
- Rubric Marines
- Plague Marines
- Noise Marines

Current collection coverage:

- Khorne Berzerkers — **20 owned** in World Eaters
- Rubric Marines — **20 owned** in Thousand Sons
- Plague Marines — none recorded
- Noise Marines — none recorded

Thus the user's CSM collection effectively has access to two additional Battleline/cult-marine pools without buying duplicate miniatures.

### Daemonic Pact

CSM can also draw from the Chaos Daemons collection, up to **500 pts at Strike Force**.

The physical daemon collection contains **340 bodies**.

The Battleline-ratio gate matters:

- Khorne — supported by Bloodletters
- Tzeentch — supported by Horrors
- Slaanesh — supported by Daemonettes
- Nurgle — currently constrained because no Plaguebearer physical pool is recorded

### Chaos Knights

An all-CHAOS army can add either one TITANIC Chaos Knight or up to three War Dogs. No Chaos Knights physical pool is currently recorded, so this is rules potential only.

## 2. Chaos Daemons / Shadow Legion

Of the 15 admitted role categories:

- **13 are directly represented** by the CSM collection
- **1 additional category** — Sorcerer In Terminator Armour — has two high-confidence physical candidates in the Thousand Sons collection
- **1 category is missing** — Raptors

Directly represented categories:

- Chaos Lord
- Chaos Lord In Terminator Armour
- Chaos Lord With Jump Pack
- Chaos Terminator Squad
- Chosen
- DAMNED units
- Dark Apostle
- Havocs
- Legionaries

The magnetized Haarken body must be configured as **Chaos Lord With Jump Pack** for Shadow Legion; Haarken himself is an Epic Hero and is not admitted by the detachment.

### Sorcerer In Terminator Armour

The Thousand Sons inventory contains **2**.

The current CSM and Thousand Sons datasheets both use:
- a 40 mm base
- a force weapon
- a combi-bolter/combi-weapon physical weapon family

Therefore these two models are recorded as **high-confidence physical candidates** for the separate CSM Sorcerer In Terminator Armour datasheet.

This is physical model reuse, not importing the Thousand Sons datasheet into CSM or Shadow Legion.

## 9. Practical consequence

The user's Chaos collection should no longer be evaluated as four isolated armies.

The physical network is better represented as:

```text
                        Chaos Daemons (340)
                       /       |        \
          Daemonic Pact    Shadow Legion   god-daemon direct access
                 /             |             \
              CSM --------- shared ---------- WE / TS
              |  \             pools
              |   \------ Chaos Spawn (10)
              |
              +-- Berzerkers (20 from WE)
              +-- Rubrics (20 from TS)
              +-- TS Terminator Sorcerers (2 physical role candidates)

future:
  Death Guard <-- Nurgle daemons
  Emperor's Children <-- Slaanesh daemons
  any CHAOS army <-- Chaos Knights ally package
```

This architecture is now the basis for collection-aware roster feasibility. Faction-local body totals remain unique-body counts; cross-faction access is represented by references, not duplication.
