## Known incompleteness

The **131-model figure is not the final physical collection total**.

Direct user clarification already establishes:

- Rangers are likely present in greater numbers than the 30 represented in the export;
- Vanguard are likely present in greater numbers than the 20 represented in the export;
- at least one additional Infiltrator Princeps/leader body exists;
- at least one additional Ruststalker Princeps/leader body exists.

Therefore the current known physical minimum is **133 models**, while the final Adeptus Mechanicus total remains unknown until the next physical count pass.

## Loadout boundary

The New Recruit file records selected weapon configurations. Those are retained as source configuration evidence only.

They are **not yet treated as verified physical loadouts or bit/component pools**. A later revision will record assembled weapons, magnetization/interchangeability and spare components separately.

The canonical normalized inventory is `current.json`.
